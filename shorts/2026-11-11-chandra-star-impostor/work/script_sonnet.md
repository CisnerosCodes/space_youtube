Title: Chandra Finds Hidden Partner of Star AT 2016blu (Nov 9)
> A star 30 million light-years away has burst at least 27 times, and survived.
> Its outbursts resemble supernova explosions.
> Why? Chandra found it at least 100 times brighter in X-rays than in 2001 to 2002.
> The best explanation is a hidden companion, either a stellar-mass black hole, or a neutron star.
> It pulls material from the star every 113 days.
> This is the first X-ray detection of one of this class of erupting stars.
> Next, Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth on Wednesday, November 18.
