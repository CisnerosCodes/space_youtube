## Judgment calls
- The Sonnet draft said "NASA did not rebuild that shield". The source never says that, so line 3 now says only what the page says: the trajectory was modified for Artemis II after the Artemis I findings.
- The draft said "Same shield, smarter flight path". Artemis II flew a different physical shield; cut.
- "factor of ten" is about cracking and char loss together, not spot counts; the script uses only the spot counts (9 and 103).
- "mid-70s" is Fahrenheit (the page says "mid-70 degree Fahrenheit range"); the on-screen data line says °F.
- "39 times the speed of sound" is the maximum velocity; the narration says "came home at", which is the reentry phase the page describes.
- The page is NASA's own assessment (single source); laser scans and sample tests continue. The script does not claim the work is finished.
