# Voice test v2: MMX (Launch Day), trimmed to about 35 seconds

Same sources as script_mmx.md (Launch Library 2, JAXA ISAS MMX page).

> Monday, Japan tries something no spacecraft has ever done: bring home a piece of a Martian moon.
> The mission is MMX, and it is headed for Phobos.
> The plan: orbit Phobos, land, scoop up a sample, and fly it back to Earth. The first material ever returned from the Mars system.
> Why? Nobody knows where Mars got its two moons. A sample could crack that.
> It even carries a tiny French and German rover called IDEFIX.
> Liftoff is Monday, October nineteenth, on an H3 rocket. We will show you the launch.

Character direction: a young, excited, nerdy guy in his early twenties, a little scrawny, talking fast because he genuinely loves this; warm, curious, slightly breathless on the big facts. Cat hint (subtle): a faint purring softness on a few r sounds and a light, curious upward lilt at the ends of some phrases. Never meow, never a cartoon voice.
