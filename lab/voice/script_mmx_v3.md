# Script v3: MMX (Launch Day), creator style, about 33 seconds

> Mars has a moon that is falling. Slowly.
> Phobos drops about six feet closer to Mars every hundred years. In fifty million years, it either crashes into Mars, or shatters into a ring.
> And on Monday, Japan launches a mission to go grab a piece of it.
> It is called MMX. Land on Phobos, scoop up a sample, fly it home. No mission has ever brought back a sample from Mars or its moons.
> Why bother? Because nobody knows where these moons came from. That sample could finally tell us.
> Liftoff is Monday, on Japan's H3 rocket. Follow, and we will show you the launch.

Writing rules used: hook with a surprising true fact in the first 2 seconds; one idea per line; "you"-level plain words; a question that the next line answers; a concrete stake; end with what happens next and a reason to follow.
