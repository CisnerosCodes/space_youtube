# Voice test script: MMX (Launch Day), about 40 seconds

Series: LAUNCH DAY. Narration (read verbatim by every voice):

> On Monday, Japan tries something no spacecraft has ever done: bring back a piece of a Martian moon.
> The mission is called MMX, and it is headed for Phobos.
> The plan: settle into orbit around Phobos, land, scoop up a sample, and fly it home to Earth.
> It would be the first material ever returned from the Mars system.
> Why Phobos? Scientists still do not know where Mars got its two moons. A sample can help answer that.
> MMX also carries IDEFIX, a small French and German rover.
> Right now, a signal from Mars takes almost thirteen minutes to reach us.
> Liftoff is Monday, October nineteenth, on Japan's H3 rocket. We will show you the launch.

Delivery direction (Fish `instructions`): curious, clear science explainer; brisk but unhurried; warm; slight lift on the questions; no announcer voice.
