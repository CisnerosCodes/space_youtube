The diagram carries any beat that no real photo depicts, so the picture never claims something it does not show.

- **Consumer provides:** the beat's subject (a burn, a path, a size comparison), its labels, and any number it carries.
- **Build:** `ink` ground; flat line art in `amber` at `stroke-diagram` (4px at every camera scale); dashed lines for paths already flown; event markers as filled amber circles, spacecraft as small filled paper squares; labels in the `diagram-label` style in `paper`. A number above it uses `giant-number` in `amber` with a `data` line under it (unit in `muted`).
- **Required:** the credit slot names the source of the facts on the beat (`Source: NASA`). No "not to scale" or other filler labels.
- **Motion:** the camera moves continuously across the drawing and paths draw in on `enter`; the amber dot that carries the story can become a marker in it.
- **Do not:** shade, glow, add textures or 3D, or draw anything that could pass for a photo.
