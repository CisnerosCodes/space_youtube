The series kicker names which of the four series a Short belongs to and stays on screen for the whole Short.

- **Consumer provides:** the series (`LAUNCH DAY`, `JUST DISCOVERED`, `HOW DOES THAT WORK`, `BY THE NUMBERS`).
- **Build:** a square `ink` box (padding 8px 16px 8px 12px), the series mark (28 x 28, `amber`, from the Series assets) then `gap-mark-label`, then the name in the `kicker` style in `paper`.
- **Place:** x 80, y 246 on every frame of every Short; under the ticks.
- **NEXT card:** the same box in the same place reads `NEXT ON <SERIES>`.
- **Do:** keep it identical every day; it is the channel's recognition signal.
- **Do not:** recolor the mark per series, animate the box, or move it between shots.
