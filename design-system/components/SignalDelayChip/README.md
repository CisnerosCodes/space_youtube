The signal delay chip states the real light travel time between Earth and the body in the story; it is the channel's signature device.

- **Consumer provides:** the body and its one-way light time, computed from a real distance for the story's date (the Moon is 1.3 s; Mars varies by date and must be computed, never guessed).
- **Build:** a square `ink` box (padding 8px 16px 8px 12px): the echo mark at 20px tall, `gap-mark-label`, "SIGNAL DELAY" in `paper`, two spaces, the value in `amber`, all in the `chip` style.
- **Place:** x 80, y 302, on the beat about that body only.
- **Motion:** the chip cuts in 4 frames after the beat cut; the echo ring lands exactly the real delay later (Moon: 39 frames), so the delay is felt, not only read.
- **Do not:** show a value without a computed source, or use the chip on a beat about Earth orbit.
